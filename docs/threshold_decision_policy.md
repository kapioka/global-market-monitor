# Threshold Decision Policy

Updated: 2026-05-15 JST

## Roles

- `risk_line_thresholds_active.json`: operational threshold set used for final action.
- `risk_line_thresholds_proposed.json`: diagnostic candidate set.
- `candidate_v2`: diagnostic/certification candidate generated by replay policy.
- certified rules: the only non-active rules that may affect final action in the future.

## Current v0.7.0 Policy

- operational set: `active`
- proposed status: `hold`
- candidate_v2 status: `diagnostic_only`
- active thresholds changed: `false`
- proposed/candidate_v2 affects final action: `false`
- rule-level certification: diagnostic / future adoption only

## Certification Rules

A threshold signal can affect final action only when all conditions are met:

- confidence is `high` or explicitly accepted `medium`
- `allow_final_action: true`
- `allow_extreme_stage: true` when used for an extreme stage
- source is not `fallback_review`
- confidence is not `low` or `not_evaluable`
- historical replay shows no clear degradation
- overblocking does not increase materially
- normal to extreme transitions are explainable by multi-family evidence

## Isolation Rules

- `source: fallback_review` is diagnostic only.
- `confidence: low` is diagnostic only.
- `confidence: not_evaluable` is diagnostic only.
- Missing metadata on proposed/candidate rules is treated conservatively as diagnostic only.
- Active rules keep backward-compatible operational behavior.

## Rule-Level Certification

v0.7.1 candidates evaluate proposed thresholds by `indicator:threshold_type` rule id instead of adopting the whole proposed set. Each rule can become `certified`, `conditional`, `diagnostic_only`, `hold`, `reject`, or `not_evaluable`.

Current operating rule:

- certified rules do not automatically affect final action in the v0.7.x line.
- final action remains controlled by active thresholds plus reliability policy.
- `fallback_review` rules cannot be certified.
- buy_window prevention is not considered proven while historical buy_window count is 0.
- oil-family overlap, especially `CL=F` / `BZ=F`, is treated as a blocking reason until longer replay evidence exists.

## Current Reason

Proposed and candidate_v2 thresholds lack completed forward-return evidence and overblock the 7 active `watch` cases. `buy_window` count is 0, so buy-window false positive prevention cannot be evaluated.
