# v0.8.48 Global Risk Logic Confidence Audit

## Goal

Audit the existing global risk-line logic without changing thresholds or final decision behavior. The purpose is to make the confidence boundary visible: which rules are operational, which rules are diagnostic-only, and how composite risk score and trigger path relate.

## Current Scope

The core risk-line monitor is a US/global market stress layer. It covers:

- equity stress: `SPY`
- credit stress: `HYG`, `LQD`, `HYG/LQD`
- volatility stress: `^VIX`, `^MOVE`
- rates stress: `^TNX`
- oil and commodity inflation stress: `CL=F`, `BZ=F`
- global dollar stress: `DX-Y.NYB`

Japan-resident FX context such as `USDJPY=X` and `EURJPY=X` is supplemental. It is not a substitute for `DX-Y.NYB` inside the global risk-line logic.

## Confidence Inventory

The active risk-line threshold payload contains 30 threshold rules.

Using `threshold_metadata.metadata_for_payload()`:

- `fallback_review`: 10 rules
- `not_evaluable`: 11 rules
- `historical_quantile`: 9 rules
- final-action eligible rules: 2
- extreme-stage eligible rules: 2

Confidence buckets:

- `fallback_review`: diagnostic-only
- `not_evaluable`: insufficient completed forward-return evidence
- `low`: weak evidence or review status prevents final-action use
- `medium`: reviewable historical evidence, currently the only eligible confidence bucket in this payload

## DXY vs JPY FX

`DX-Y.NYB` is used as a global dollar stress indicator in the US/global risk-line family.

`USDJPY=X` and `EURJPY=X` are used for Japan-resident or JPY-converted asset context. They explain currency translation effects for local investors, but they do not replace `DX-Y.NYB` in the core risk-line monitor.

## Composite Score and Trigger Path

The composite risk score summarizes weighted pressure across the available risk indicators. The trigger path records the material contributors that explain the current stage:

- indicator rows: individual stressed indicators
- overlay rows: persistence or combined-condition rules
- composite score row: the aggregate stress threshold when it contributes

This audit displays the relationship without changing the stage calculation.
