# Buy Decision Card review

v0.7.2 adds a Buy Decision Card to make the current buy-decision path easier to read.

The card shows:

- final_action
- market_raw_action
- risk_adjusted_action
- buy_readiness_score
- primary blocker
- unlock conditions

This is explanatory only. It does not change final_action, reliability policy, threshold JSON, fx_soft_cap adoption, or regime-aware policy adoption.

`buy_candidate` remains a candidate label, not a buy instruction.
