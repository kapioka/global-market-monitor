# v0.8.46 Domestic Market Metrics Layer

## Purpose

This checkpoint adds a display-only domestic market metrics layer for
Japan-resident ETF and FX series. The layer converts acquired price series into
readable metrics so domestic danger context does not rely mainly on acquisition
logs or candidate presence.

## Covered series

- Japan equity: `1306.T`, `1321.T`, `EWJ`
- JPY bond proxy: `2510.T`
- Japan REIT: `1343.T`
- JPY gold proxy: `1540.T`
- FX context: `USDJPY=X`, `EURJPY=X`
- Reference-only comparison groups: `AGG`, `TIP`, `LQD`, `HYG`, `GLD`, `GC=F`

`1540.T` is kept separate from `GLD` and `GC=F`. JPY bond metrics are not mixed
with foreign bond metrics, and Japan REIT metrics are not mixed with US REIT
metrics.

## Metric fields

Each row records:

- current value
- 1w / 4w / 12w percent change
- 12w momentum
- max drawdown
- z-score when enough history exists
- trend label
- volatility label
- data quality
- sample / partial / unavailable flags
- limitations such as `missing_series`, `insufficient_history`, or
  `insufficient_zscore_history`

## Domestic danger integration

Domestic danger context now consumes these metrics through multi-asset candidate
rows and FX metric rows.

- Acquisition log presence alone is shown as normal/observed context and does
  not create watch or caution.
- Missing JGB, CPI, BOJ, or price metrics remain limitations.
- Domestic equity can become watch/caution only from weak or sharply declining
  price metrics.
- JPY bond and Japan REIT caution requires rate pressure plus weak price
  metrics.
- FX caution uses `USDJPY=X` and `EURJPY=X`; DXY is not used as a substitute.

## Report display

The multi-asset and domestic danger report sections show compact metrics when
available:

- latest value
- 4w change
- 12w change
- trend label
- max drawdown
- limitations

When metrics are missing, the report keeps beginner-readable limitation wording
instead of turning absence into a risk signal.

## Non-impact boundary

This layer is display/context only.

- no `final_action` change
- no `buy_readiness_score` change
- no threshold JSON change
- no reliability policy change
- no buy blocker/card logic change
- no new data source or dependency
- no generated report, cache, manual source, or release artifact committed
